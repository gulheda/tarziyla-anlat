## English (App Store primary language: English (U.S.))

**Name (30):** Tarzıyla Anlat
**Subtitle (30):** Funny charades party game
**Promotional text (170):** Draw a word, draw a style, act it out! The funny charades-style party game for friends. English and Türkçe, free, no ads.
**Keywords (100):** charades,party game,word game,guessing game,friends,acting game,group game,funny,family game,offline
**Google Play short description (80):** Funny charades-style party game for friends. Free, no ads, English & Türkçe.

**Description (4000):**

Tarzıyla Anlat is the funniest charades-style party game for your friend group. Free, offline, and no ads.

How it works
• The player whose turn it is takes the phone.
• Two cards appear: the WORD card says what to act out, the STYLE card says how.
• Try "Penguin" + "Talk like your grandma", or "Sandwich" + "Meow every now and then while talking".
• Describe the word without saying it. Everyone else guesses.
• Swipe right for "Got it", left for "Pass".

Two game modes
• Classic: relaxed party mode. The guesser and the actor both score.
• Competitive: teams race the clock. One point for every card you get through before time runs out.

Why you'll love it
• 600+ single words and 200+ hilarious styles in English, and the same in Turkish, for tens of thousands of combinations.
• Every card is written for its language, not machine-translated.
• 2 to 8 players or teams. Pick 30, 60 or 90 second turns and 1 to 10 rounds.
• One tap per turn: the 3-2-1 countdown starts by itself.
• The clock pauses automatically when you leave the app.
• Confetti, a podium and silly verdicts after every round.
• English and Türkçe, chosen from your device language or in Settings.
• Open source (MIT). No accounts, no ads, no tracking. No data is collected.

Gather your friends and let the show begin!

## Category, age rating, privacy / Kategori, yaş, gizlilik
- **Category:** Games > Card (primary), Games > Word (secondary). Google Play: Casual > Party, or Word.
- **Age rating:** answer the questionnaire honestly: no violence, profanity, gambling, user-generated content or web access. Accept the result.
- **Privacy:** App Store "Data Not Collected". Google Play Data safety: "No data collected, no data shared". Privacy policy URL: host `store/privacy-policy.html` publicly (see `YAYIN_KONTROL_LISTESI.md`).
- **App Review notes:** No login or internet needed. Choose "Classic" or "Competitive" on the home screen, then tap "Start the show!". The countdown starts by itself.
- **Screenshots:** 6.9" iPhone (1320 x 2868) and Android phone. Suggested order: home, setup, play, result, podium. Ready-made Android screenshots are in `docs/screenshots/` (English and Turkish).
